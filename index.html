WHAT IS STILL NEEDED

Connect a real AI model

The front end alone cannot reliably determine whether a picture was generated by AI. That requires a trained image classifier or detection model, measured on relevant data, and an integration endpoint.

ⓘ

Do not use the sample percentage as evidence that an image is real or AI-generated. The app currently runs without a real detection model.
